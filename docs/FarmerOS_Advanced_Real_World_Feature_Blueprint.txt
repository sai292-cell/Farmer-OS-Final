FARMEROS — ADVANCED REAL-WORLD AGRICULTURE PRODUCT BLUEPRINT
===============================================================

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
----------------
This document is the new master product direction for FarmerOS.

Important:
This is NOT a list of features we have already developed.
It is an advanced future-facing product blueprint designed around real farmer
problems, modern agriculture, precision farming, AI, geospatial technology,
market access, farm economics, services, and trustworthy digital advisory.

FarmerOS should be designed as a FARM OPERATING SYSTEM rather than just an
agriculture chatbot.

PRODUCT VISION
--------------
FarmerOS should help a farmer answer five questions every day:

1. What is happening in my farm?
2. What should I do today?
3. What risk may happen next?
4. Where can I buy/sell/get services at a better value?
5. How can I increase income while reducing unnecessary cost and risk?

The platform should combine farmer-entered data, verified public data, maps,
weather, market information, expert knowledge, AI, and optional sensors.

IMPORTANT REAL-WORLD DIRECTION
------------------------------
India's current digital agriculture direction includes Farmer Registry,
Geo-Referenced Village Maps, Crop Sown Registry, geospatial data, weather,
soil, water availability, yield modelling, and AI/Big Data. FarmerOS should
therefore be designed to complement this ecosystem rather than pretend to
replace government records.

Modern agriculture is increasingly combining AI, IoT, remote sensing, GIS,
satellite imagery, drones, machinery data, and precision agriculture.

FarmerOS should follow the same direction while remaining usable by a small
farmer with a normal smartphone.

REAL-WORLD DIFFERENTIATORS
==========================

1. FARM-FIRST, NOT CHATBOT-FIRST
FarmerOS should be built around a real digital farm record. The AI is only one
layer.

2. ONE FARMER → MANY FARMS → MANY PLOTS
The data model should support real agricultural complexity.

3. EVIDENCE + HISTORY
The system remembers what happened instead of starting from zero every season.

4. DECISION SUPPORT, NOT FALSE CERTAINTY
AI should explain, show confidence, cite sources, and escalate when necessary.

5. NET PROFIT, NOT JUST PRICE
Market intelligence should consider price, transport, quantity, timing, and
known costs.

6. LOCAL + PERSONAL
Advice should consider location, crop, soil, weather, farm history, and
farmer language.

7. HUMAN + AI
Experts, experienced farmers, extension workers, and AI should complement
one another.

8. OFFLINE-FIRST
A farmer should not lose important farm records because internet connectivity
is poor.

9. DATA OWNERSHIP & PRIVACY
Land, financial, crop, and personal information must not be treated as public
data by default.

10. EXPLAINABLE PREDICTIONS
A prediction should show what information was used and what could make the
prediction wrong.

11. OUTCOME TRACKING
FarmerOS should compare recommendations with what actually happened. This
creates a feedback loop for improving future models.

12. FARMER ECONOMICS FIRST
Every major feature should answer:
"Does this save cost, reduce risk, save time, increase income, or improve
resource efficiency?"

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT RULES
============================

1. Never invent market prices.
2. Never invent government scheme eligibility.
3. Never present an AI image screening as a laboratory-confirmed diagnosis.
4. Never provide unsupported pesticide/fungicide/herbicide doses.
5. Never hide uncertainty.
6. Always show data date/time for time-sensitive information.
7. Prefer official/authoritative sources for government and agricultural
   recommendations.
8. Keep farmer land and financial data private by default.
9. Allow expert escalation for high-risk agricultural decisions.
10. Log important AI recommendations and corrections.
11. Measure whether recommendations actually helped farmers.
12. Design for low-end smartphones and weak internet.
13. Keep important features usable in Marathi/Hindi/local languages.
14. Do not build fake "live" features using dummy data in the production
   product.
15. If real-time data is unavailable, clearly label the information as
   historical, estimated, simulated, or unavailable.
16. Separate actual observations from predictions.
17. Never guarantee yield, profit, disease outcome, or market price.
18. Build modularly so each feature can be independently tested and replaced.

1. LAND MEASUREMENT & DIGITAL BOUNDARY RECORD
----------------------------------------------

Purpose:
Create a reliable digital record of the farmer's land measurement and
boundary geometry.

Features:

A. Digital Land Measurement
- Measure farm area using an interactive map.
- Support acres, hectares, square meters, and local units where required.
- Save the measured area permanently with the farm record.
- Allow multiple plots under one farmer.

B. Boundary Polygon
- Farmer marks the complete farm boundary on a map.
- Store all boundary GPS points.
- Display the exact saved polygon whenever the farmer opens the farm.

C. Boundary Side Measurement
- Calculate the length of each boundary side.
- Example:
  North = 120 m
  East = 85 m
  South = 118 m
  West = 87 m

D. Boundary Straightness / Alignment Record
- Store the actual GPS line/segments forming each side of the farm.
- Show whether a boundary side is approximately straight, curved, or
  irregular based on the recorded GPS points.
- Display the original measurement rather than silently changing it.
- Allow the farmer to re-measure and save a new survey version.

E. Boundary Version History
- Keep previous boundary measurements.
- Store:
  Measurement date
  GPS data
  Area
  Boundary points
  Photos
  Notes
- Never overwrite historical evidence without retaining the old version.

F. Boundary Evidence Pack
For every farm boundary, allow:
- North-side photos
- South-side photos
- East-side photos
- West-side photos
- Videos
- GPS coordinates
- Measurement date/time
- Farmer notes
- Optional witness/expert details

G. Boundary Comparison
- Compare two saved boundary measurements.
- Highlight where recorded lines/areas differ.
- Show the difference numerically.

H. Boundary Sharing
- Farmer can generate a controlled read-only map/report for selected people.

I. Dispute Documentation Mode
- Create a chronological evidence package containing:
  Land measurement
  Boundary polygon
  GPS points
  Photos
  Videos
  Dates
  Historical versions
  Notes

IMPORTANT LEGAL LIMIT:
FarmerOS must describe this as a digital measurement/evidence and recordkeeping
system. It must NOT claim that a GPS measurement inside the app legally proves
ownership, title, or a legally surveyed boundary. For a legal land dispute,
official cadastral/survey records and authorized survey professionals remain
the authoritative source.


2. CROP WATERING / IRRIGATION SCHEDULE
---------------------------------------

Purpose:

IMPORTANT:

recommendations as decision support and show the factors used.

6. CROP-WISE PROFIT & LOSS
--------------------------

B. Total Crop Cost

Total Cost =
Seeds
+ Fertilizer
+ Crop Protection
+ Labour
+ Irrigation
+ Machinery
+ Transport
+ Other Costs

C. Crop Profit

Profit =
Revenue - Total Crop Cost

D. Crop Loss

If Total Crop Cost > Revenue:
Loss = Total Crop Cost - Revenue

E. Profit Per Acre

Profit Per Acre =
Crop Profit / Cultivated Area

F. Crop Comparison

Example:
